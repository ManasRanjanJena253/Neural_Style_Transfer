Neural Style Transfer: Where Deep Learning Meets Art
This project uses Neural Style Transfer to blend the content of everyday images with the style of iconic artworks—transforming simple visuals into stunning digital art. While the model itself is GPU-intensive and cannot run live here, the showcased outputs are generated offline and curated to highlight the magic of deep neural networks in recreating artistic expression.
